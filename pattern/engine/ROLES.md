# Role discovery and maintained knowledge

First candidate. Mandatory before staffing, at every permitted depth.

Use one canonical project role index, linked by the shared bootstrap and recorded in PROJECT, to discover maintained
responsibilities. The index can link generic pattern roles and organization or
project-specific roles across several projects. Generic Director and Request Manager
instructions stay reusable; the mapping selects applicable local knowledge rather
than rewriting those generic roles for every team or domain.

Each index entry needs a stable role ID, responsibility and selection cues,
canonical definition locator, applicability, required shared guardrails, runbooks,
and reviewed learnings, owning maintainer, its assignment role (`worker` or
`director`), and any authority or route constraints. Keep it quick to scan; load
definitions and references for selected work rather than every role's history.
Keep project authority and shared gates startup-required. Label task references
with the affected-work trigger and action references with the before-use trigger;
unqualified mandatory references load with the requiring document, at startup
when it is startup-required. Deferred documents bring their unqualified transitive
requirements with them before the trigger. Deferral changes timing, never
applicability or authority. Do not rephase project-owned
requirements during engine replacement without reviewed preservation mapping.
Project-wide applicable guardrails apply to everyone independently of role
selection; specialist references add to them and cannot bypass them.

## Selection knowledge and execution knowledge

The staffer needs enough maintained knowledge to select and bound the assignment:
responsibility, applicability, assignment role, ownership, authority and route
constraints, shared guards, and the canonical reference/trigger map. Keep this
selection package in the index and selected standalone definition, with explicit
links to any further selection-required guards. Load that package completely
before assigning. A selector must expose every constraint that could change who
may be assigned, the scope, reserved decisions, or the permitted route; it cannot
hide such a constraint in a deferred executor manual.

The executor needs the full instructions for its actual work. The staffer need
not eagerly read every executor task manual merely to assign it: supply its exact
locator and trigger, and require the actor to load it completely before affected
work. This is a distinction between responsibilities, not permission to skip
authority or the actor's startup. Requirements addressed to the executor remain
that actor's complete reading duty; selection does not make the staffer execute
that role's startup or load its craft manuals. Existing unqualified actor
requirements still load for that actor with their requiring document under
PATTERN. An ambiguous reference that
may contain selection/authority guards must be read before staffing or resolved
through reviewed separation; do not assume it is only craft guidance. Existing
explicit staffer read requirements also remain binding until reviewed integration
preserves their constraints in this selection package. No separate registry or
second role table is needed.

## Required discovery sequence

1. Identify the task's work and affected project and resources.
2. Read the canonical index and select all applicable maintained roles and reference
   layers. A role definition may compose shared guardrails with a project runbook.
3. Load the selected definition and its complete selection/authority package before
   writing the assignment. Resolve unclear guards; identify the executor's full
   startup, task and action references with exact triggers. Confirm existence and
   coherence with explicit authority without loading unrelated execution manuals.
4. Supply their exact canonical locators in the bounded [assignment](ASSIGNMENT.md),
   with source, scope, acceptance evidence, model/effort selection, Recorder route,
   and identity.
5. Require the actor to complete and acknowledge startup, then load task/action
   references before their explicit triggers. Record acknowledgment through the
   manager/Recorder route when actually received, in the next appropriate batch;
   one sufficient startup acknowledgment needs no separate ritual for each manual.
   The same rule applies to any descendants.

If a matching maintained role exists, do not substitute a generic engineer, tester,
or customer communicator. If the index is missing, a relevant reference is
unavailable, or applicable roles conflict, return the affected staffing decision for
resolution. Do not quietly infer a generic fallback. Explicit resolution may supply a
missing definition, select the applicable layer, or authorize a bounded temporary
role; retain that decision. Unaffected work can proceed.

## Maintain standalone definitions and one index

The canonical project-owned index is the bootstrap's single discovery route;
PROJECT records the same locator. Neither the startup hook nor this guide keeps
another live role table. Package definitions available for registration are
[Director](DIRECTOR.md), [Request Manager](REQUEST_MANAGER.md),
[Recorder](RECORDER.md), and optional [bounded analysis](ANALYSIS.md).
Register real applicable entries during installation, with the fields above.
Shared [worker boundaries](WORKER.md) are a contract, not a fallback specialist.

When work becomes recurring, create or revise a standalone maintained definition
with its responsibility, applicability, `worker`/`director` assignment role,
authority boundary, phased required references, and maintainer. Move exact existing
specialist instructions into that owning file or referenced contract; distill
shared project authority only when its meaning is preserved. Update the same
canonical index before staffing, check the bootstrap reaches it and its definition
and references exist, then require the actor's acknowledgment. Renames and
retirement also update callers and index entries. Do not append recurring role
duties or an abstract of the pattern to globally loaded startup instructions.
An unindexed file or illustrative entry is not a discoverable maintained role.

## Improve the system's knowledge

Preserve meaningful friction, evidence, and a supported cause separately from
speculation. Propose the smallest correction to the owning role or runbook
maintainer. After review and an authorized update, retain its revision or evidence
link in the index or canonical reference. Future assignments load that corrected
reference; evaluate its next relevant use. Do not make each fresh actor rediscover
the last team's lessons, or turn every anecdote into a universal rule.

At meaningful outcome closure, review the touched knowledge path as part of the
existing evaluation: keep useful roles, refresh selection cues, canonical artifact
pointers and accepted facts, and correct or retire what this outcome showed stale.
Retain dated evidence and superseded reasoning; move stale runtime assertions out
of live instructions into the existing receipt/history instead of deleting them or
turning them into defaults. Keep enduring authority distinct from observations.
Make only authorized edits through the owning maintainer/worker route; carry an
unresolved proposal forward without inventing an approval gate for delivery.
Evaluate the correction at its next relevant use. This is local upkeep of touched
knowledge, not a role census, recurring service, new framework, or arbitrary size cap.

A role is a maintained responsibility and knowledge package. It is not a live agent,
a model size, a permission level, a department instance, or a delegation license.
