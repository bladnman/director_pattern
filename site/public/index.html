How the work moves

Many pieces.
One accountable conversation.

The director stays responsible for the integrated result. A worker’s return is something to evaluate; the director decides whether it meets the request or needs another pass.

An illustrative project request↗
“Add a settings page,
update the guide,
and check it on a phone.”
  1. 01
    Make each outcome visible

    The page, the guide, and the phone check each have a clear owner and acceptance criteria.

  2. 02
    Assign the work, keep the context

    Workers execute bounded assignments. The recorder preserves the original request, later steering, and current state.

  3. 03
    Judge the whole result

    The director checks evidence, requests fixes, and accounts for anything still waiting. Passing a build and checking a real phone are separate claims.

Continuity

Tracked by default.
Lean when you choose.

New installations retain requests and unfinished work by default. Lean is an explicit option with a smaller retention promise. An existing tracker stays canonical.

Roles

Clear assignments.
No forced org chart.

A new project conversation defaults to its director. An explicit assignment sets the role and scope; inherited history does not. Workers execute, the director judges, and the recorder handles routine state.

An action needs you, such as authentication or a physical check.

📋
A periodic situation-report block, easy to scan or skip.

📋 SitRep

Use a periodic overview after a quiet stretch of ongoing work or when several developments need a combined view. Ordinary replies and brief progress do not need a full SitRep; meaningful updates already satisfy the cadence. Communicate meaningful changes promptly, keep silence under five minutes, and respect stricter host timing with concise updates. Reports keep held work and next proof visible while supervision continues; saved status alone does not prove activity. The checked director keeps its 🎬 response prefix.

Keep it current, deliberately

Update when
you ask.

Installations do not silently update at startup. Published GitHub main is the upstream source of truth; your agent resolves a reviewed revision to an exact commit.

The update protocol backs up managed files, compares changes, preserves local adaptations and private state, and records the new revision after verification. A tracker migration is a separate decision.
