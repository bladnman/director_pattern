How the work moves

Many pieces.
One accountable conversation.

The Director stays accountable to you. Each complete outcome has one Request Manager, who evaluates what specialists return and submits an integrated result. That submission is something to review, not automatic acceptance: the Director decides whether it meets the request or needs another pass.

An illustrative project request↗
“Add a settings page,
update the guide,
and check it on a phone.”
  1. 01
    One outcome, one owner

    The page, the guide, and the phone check are parts of one finishable result, so one manager owns all of it and its completion obligations from the start. Separate managers are for outcomes you can accept independently.

  2. 02
    Assign the work, keep the context

    The manager picks the project’s matching roles and gives specialists bounded assignments. The recorder preserves the original request, later steering, and current state.

  3. 03
    Judge the whole result

    The manager checks returns and orders rework; the Director reviews the integrated result and accounts for anything still waiting. Passing a build and checking a real phone are separate claims.

Continuity

Tracked by default.
Lean when you choose.

New installations retain requests and unfinished work by default. Lean is an explicit option with a smaller retention promise. An existing tracker stays canonical. Files keep continuity; they install no runtime and cannot keep an agent running.

Roles

Clear assignments.
No forced org chart.

Explicit assignments select a maintained role definition to read before action. An unassigned project conversation defaults to its Director; incomplete delegated assignments seek clarification. Inherited history grants no role. A small outcome can go straight to a worker. Staffing uses your project’s role index and model options, so matching specialist rules apply instead of generic helpers.

📋
A periodic situation-report block, easy to scan or skip.

📋 SitRep

Use a periodic overview after a quiet stretch of ongoing work or when several developments need a combined view. Ordinary replies and brief progress do not need a full SitRep; meaningful updates already satisfy the cadence. Communicate meaningful changes promptly, keep silence under five minutes, and respect stricter host timing with concise updates. Reports keep held work and next proof visible while supervision continues; saved status alone does not prove activity. The checked Director keeps its 🎬 response prefix.

Keep it current, deliberately

Update when
you ask.

Installations do not silently update at startup. Published GitHub main is the upstream source of truth; your agent resolves a reviewed revision to an exact commit.

The update protocol backs up managed files, compares changes, preserves local adaptations and project state, and records the new revision after verification. Your role index, model mapping, and identity roster belong to your project and are not replaced. The whole startup chain is audited during upgrades too, so old specialist duties and competing defaults do not remain globally loaded. A tracker migration is a separate decision.
