2 · What lives where

The block routes. Each card carries one kind of work.

“Open before” is the proposed trigger. The card's content is retrieved on demand; its name in the block does not mean it has already been read.

01

Badge · check and 🎬

Open after a failed check, or when changing how instructions load. Explains what the check claims and when to retry.
02

Recovery · held work

INTENDED · NOT YET DEMONSTRATED
The prose trigger says to read it at a Director's first project action after compaction or resume. The scratch recipe installs no host hook or settings to prompt that read; both observed candidate sessions missed the card. Nov 10 update: offline hook plumbing passed synthetic input only. In the native attempt, PreCompact fired, but provider quota stopped the task before output; /compact produced no boundary. Native recovery remains unverified.
03

Requests · capture and steer

Open before capturing, splitting, steering, or running a store command. Covers request lifecycle and state work.
04

Staffing · assign work

Open before staffing, briefing, or setting tier and effort. Covers role choice, scope, authority, and checks.
05

Supervision · evaluate work

Open while supervising, evaluating a return, or ending a turn with work out. Covers SitReps and completion.
06

Knowledge · learn and maintain

Open for task knowledge, recording a correction, or outcome closure. Covers selectors and upkeep.
07

Update check · update authority

Open when the last daily check is older than 24 hours (or none is recorded), or when discussing an update.
08

Privacy · exact sources

Open before first exact-source capture or changing Git tracking. Covers visibility and ignore choices.

3 · What the route leaves unresolved

Old files can stay installed and still become unreachable.

The scratch recipe retained old startup files to satisfy the shipped checker's file hashes. The candidate block did not send the Director to START or DIRECTOR; Operations was named only as detail in selected cases.

Still on disk

Not the default route

START, DIRECTOR and OPERATIONS remained unchanged in the scratch folder. Their presence is not proof that the candidate reads the obligations they contain.

Observed reachability miss

Recovery card unopened

The trigger is compaction or resume. New work already outstanding at conversation start may not trigger it.

Needs disposition

Several duties shift

Read order, assignment conflicts, first-use order, guard timing, update authority, role aliases and other duties need an explicit home and regression oracle.

Another mismatch: the badge card points to old startup files and says to confirm complete startup reads, while the proposed block removes those reads from the normal Director route. The candidate's checker also needed a route change that was not shipped.

4 · What was actually observed

A passing check did not mean the new cards were read.

Four live session trials: two disposable starter 3.2 controls and two scratch-candidate sessions. The control is neither this checkout's local `.director` route nor a published release. Each had one manually forced compaction on one host.

What passed

The post-break check and badge path in each trial's own setup. One initial control greeting was permission-blocked and unbadged; it passed after compaction. The other control passed initially. Both candidate greetings passed initially only with the patched checker.

What remains unproved

Card re-entry, held-work recovery, actual request/state writes, returns and SitRep cadence, reliable delegation, resumes, repeated compaction, other hosts, or a real installer/checker change.

The follow-up prompts were a status question and a small code lookup. Both setups allowed direct handling, so differing handoff counts are ambiguous. Writes were denied. Do not infer a rate or general reliability from four trials.

5 · The decision is still open

Should the startup route change—and what must the replacement prove?

A smaller first-read path is not enough. The proposed route needs an accepted home for every affected duty and an observable recovery test.

Role and authority

Who is a worker when spawned to report back? Should the roster replace role-definition reads? Should Director package acknowledgment be removed?

Check and badge

Should 🎬 stop certifying read/hash-managed startup parity? Must project guards and generated first-use happen before the check?

Triggers and missing duties

Where do assignment conflicts, role aliases, cross-project updates, and work already held at a new conversation get a reliable trigger?

Updates and size

How does project-owned setup survive updates? Are the block/card caps, over-cap badge card, and old-pointer contradictions acceptable?

Next gate: independently review the obligation map and concrete regression cases—including the observed “check passed, zero cards read” case—before any payload implementation. N1/N2/N4/N5/N6 are design choices in the proposal but absent from the scratch recipe; N3 remains unchecked.
