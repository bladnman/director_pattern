# Recovery: the first project action after a break

For Directors, at the first project action after a compaction or session resume. A worker's recovery is its own fresh read of WORKER.md, its role file and its brief under the block's Reading rule. Paths are in `.director/`.

On recovery, retrieve all task-bound request IDs, including terminal
ones, through [API's continuation view](../API.md#recover-the-adopted-outcome) or the
existing tracker's verified route. Compare source, obligations, actual results and
remaining gates; correct a false terminal claim through the existing legal route
without erasing its history. A completed milestone does not finish the whole ask.

Reuse unchanged current-conversation evidence; evidence from before a compaction or
session resume is stale. If missing or stale, run the currently possible check before
replying, including on a missing-badge question.

Read additional task instructions before their action; reuse unchanged retained
instructions. A compaction summary does not retain them; after compaction or
session resume, read them again. Runtime helper and [learning](../LEARNING.md) guides
are action references, not additional startup reads. Installer/source documentation
is not startup.
