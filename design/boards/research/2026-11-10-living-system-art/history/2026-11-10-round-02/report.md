## Why the movement feels detached

In the preserved first version, signals use paths separate from the visible connections. A packet can cross empty space while its supposed wiring sits elsewhere. Whole branch fans activate together, so the eye sees a colored state rather than a spreading local response. Record icons also appear at story-state entry, before the corresponding incoming signal has arrived. These choices weaken the connection between cause and consequence.

## Art direction: one responsive organism at different scales

Give branches distinct lengths, densities, fork depths and open spaces. Let the membrane, hubs and tips move quietly together, with their connections attached. A message should travel along the actual wiring and produce a restrained response where it arrives. Let unrelated regions remain quiet. Light should reveal the space doing the work.

Keep record timing meaningful. A packet contacts its receiver; the node visibly throbs or expands; shortly afterward its nearby record updates to preserve receipt. Connected downstream nodes work independently, with their used arms brighter than quiet arms. Completion updates that same record again before the return leaves. The owner receives the return, updates the request and then chooses the next route. Both record updates must be visible as separate events.

When the camera widens, retain the main cell's identity and reveal neighbors with different proportions and internal density. One clear exchange should establish cooperation before the wider web appears. Keep the prose and controls readable; let activity become the focal point when it occurs.

## Presenter-led pacing

Each arrow cue should begin the next step at its opening, play its local cause-and-consequence sequence, then hold the settled outcome for the next cue. A presenter should be able to reveal the story one deliberate action at a time. Continuous autoplay can remain a separate choice.
