# Executive briefing direction · November 11, 2026

The existing living-cell metaphor is the recognisable subject. This independent
version makes one changing system the visual anchor and uses short executive
claims in a quiet left column. It avoids file-name lists and decorative routes.

Palette: deep ocean `#06111d`, cool paper `#e5eff6`, signal teal `#54dfc2`,
recipient rose `#f2a5c5`, inspection violet `#b9a5ed`, evidence amber `#ebbd77`.
Georgia supplies a conversational display voice; the native sans-serif stack
keeps explanation and controls clean. The diagonal of the internal graph gives
the scene movement without treating it as a company organisation chart.

Layout: left-aligned claim and a single sentence occupy about one third of the
screen; the changing graph takes the rest. A restrained footer holds cue and
chapter controls. On narrow screens explanation sits above the whole diagram.

The first plan risked becoming a glowing technical dashboard. The revised plan
uses no metrics, no tiled feature cards, no idle particle field and no persistent
external connector web. Motion is reserved for the current presenter cue; the
scene settles and waits. The pending-message collection is deliberately a plain
list whose rows exist only when messages are written.
