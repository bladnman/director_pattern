A role is not an agent.

Written responsibilities, live execution and durable evidence do different jobs.

Moving partWhat it meansWhat it does not mean
Role definitionResponsibility, selected knowledge, references, authority and route constraints.A running actor, model ID or permission grant.
Agent instanceActual dispatched work with a handle and dated observations; supervised through evaluation.A name in a file or a stored activity sentence.
Canonical recordRequest bundle with exact source links, replayable history, obligations, gates and evidence.An automatic wakeup, a true human identity, or proof supplied evidence is correct.

Seven states retain their meaning

LocationStatesMeaning
Pendinginbox, queuedCaptured work, then a substantive plan with owner, acceptance and human gate.
Workingactive, review, blockedActual execution binding, submitted evidence awaiting evaluation, or a named unmet condition.
Archivedone, canceledAccepted scope with its gates satisfied, or an explicit non-success ending.

Submission is not acceptance. Rework, steering and reopening use legal transitions and preserve the original plan and history. Adding new work to an accepted outcome cannot leave it silently done.

A worker permitted to assign descendants must first load the operational staffing guide and selected role guards. Capability, effort, verification and honest settings evidence apply at that depth too; permission to delegate is not permission to skip discovery or independent review.

Two generic foundations

Director owns conversation, coordination and acceptance. Worker executes a bounded assignment and returns artifacts, checks and unresolved gates. Domain instructions compose with that foundation; a specialist is not a new assignment-role value.
