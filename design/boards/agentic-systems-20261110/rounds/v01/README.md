# Inside agentic systems

A connected set of five animated diagrams, created November 10, 2026.

The cell is an ongoing area of work. Its branches are example responsibilities;
one can represent an individual, a team or a subsystem. A cell can produce many
outputs. The diagrams explain a way of working without prescribing an org chart.

## Visual direction

- Deep space `#03090f`, membrane teal `#27ddc8`, luminous ice `#dbeaf7`,
  quiet slate `#8497a8`, exchange rose `#f58eab`, decision amber `#e9bd79`.
- Georgia supplies the large editorial titles; a system sans supplies the small
  functional labels. Typography remains subordinate to the moving diagram.
- An asymmetric stage gives a short explanation on the left and a large living
  cell on the right. The same role geography survives the camera move.
- Only meaningful work moves: messages travel, records change, branches light up.
  The controls pause the complete timeline. Reduced motion uses stable steps.

This is deliberately a close visual relative of the supplied cell study. The
organic double membrane and branching light are the memorable element; the
presentation frame stays quiet. The five chapters share geometry and signal
vocabulary rather than becoming five unrelated infographics.

## Open or serve

Open `presenter.html` directly, or run from this directory:

```sh
python3 -m http.server 8765
```

Then open `http://127.0.0.1:8765/presenter.html`. Everything is local; there are no
network assets or dependencies. In this project's Tinka it is also available at
`/resources/agentic-systems/presenter.html`.

Chapters: `#systems`, `#director`, `#learning`, `#communication`, `#human`.
Use Previous/Next, the stage dots or arrow keys to step. Space plays/pauses;
R replays the chapter. Home returns to its beginning. The chapter selector opens
another diagram. Full screen is optional.

## Scope of the illustrations

The Director chapter depicts current method contracts: capture intent, maintain
durable records, delegate bounded work, communicate progress, evaluate returns
and conclude against the whole request. A record icon is a conceptual durable
record, not a mandated file format.

Learning depicts the current bounded review/owner decision/later-use loop.
Expanding the cell with a new specialty illustrates an optional growth proposal:
a proposal is reviewed, implemented in maintained instructions, then used by a
later request. An Architect is optional, and the diagram promises no autonomous
background reviewer or scheduler.

The shared exchange in the communication and human chapters is a proposed
transport. Addressing, ownership, acknowledgment and evaluated return are the
intended contract; this prototype does not implement a universal bus. Human
choices remain explicit. Waiting is not approval, and an approved decision does
not grant unrelated publication or spending authority.

All work, routing and status inside the presentation are animated illustrations,
not live agents or execution evidence. The prototype is explanatory material and
does not change the installable Director Pattern.

## Prototype verification

The November 10 pass rendered all 36 states in Chrome at 1280 × 720 with no
script errors or overlapping text bands. All chapter openings were also checked
at 900 × 720 and 390 × 844. Play/pause froze the signal position and elapsed
time; previous/next, keyboard arrows and Home changed the expected step. Reduced
motion rendered stable signals. Independent conceptual review found a missing
final Quality return; distinct return/update steps were added before conclusion
in the first two diagrams and reviewed again.

These are prototype checks and independent source review. They do not establish
owner creative acceptance or validate the Director Pattern's live behavior.
