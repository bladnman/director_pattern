outputs. The diagrams explain a way of working without prescribing an org chart.

## Open or serve

Open `presenter.html` directly, or run from this directory:

```sh
python3 -m http.server 8765
```

Then open `http://127.0.0.1:8765/presenter.html`. Everything is local; there are no
network assets or dependencies. In this project's Tinka it is also available at
`/resources/agentic-systems/presenter.html`.

Chapters: `#systems`, `#director`, `#learning`, `#communication`, `#human`.

Current revision controls:

- **Next / →, Previous / ←, or a stage dot:** start the selected step at its
  beginning, play its local sequence, then hold at **Ready** for the next cue.
- **R:** replay the current step. **Home:** cue the chapter's first step.
- **Play all / Space:** continue through the remaining steps in this chapter.
  **Pause / Continue** stops and resumes that playback. Chapters do not advance
  automatically into one another.
- **Pause motion / Resume motion:** freeze or resume anatomy, camera, packets,
  record updates and story together. Selecting a new cue resumes motion for it.
- **Chapter selector or chapter link:** open the chosen chapter or linked step
  at its beginning. Step links retain the story's preceding settled records.
- **Reduced motion:** show each selected step's settled pose immediately.

Full screen is optional. These controls describe the current implementation;
complete revised playback and creative acceptance remain under review.

## Scope of the illustrations

The Director chapter depicts current method contracts: capture intent, maintain
durable records, delegate bounded work, communicate progress, evaluate returns
and conclude against the whole request. A record icon is a conceptual durable
record, not a mandated file format.

Learning depicts the current bounded review/owner decision/later-use loop.
Expanding the cell with a new specialty illustrates an optional growth proposal:
a proposal is reviewed, implemented in maintained instructions, then used by a
later request. An Architect is optional, and the diagram promises no autonomous
background reviewer or scheduler.

The shared exchange in the communication and human chapters is a proposed
transport. Addressing, ownership, acknowledgment and evaluated return are the
intended contract; this prototype does not implement a universal bus. Human
choices remain explicit. Waiting is not approval, and an approved decision does
not grant unrelated publication or spending authority.

All work, routing and status inside the presentation are animated illustrations,
not live agents or execution evidence. The prototype is explanatory material and
does not change the installable Director Pattern.
