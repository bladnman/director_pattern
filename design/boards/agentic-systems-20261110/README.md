## What the chapters explain

An ongoing Fieldwork domain can produce many plans, tools and reports. One two-site survey package provides a concrete through-line: prepare a plan and tools, check the evidence, resolve a returned gap and conclude against the original conditions. Branches can represent agents, teams or maintained responsibilities; the example does not prescribe an organization chart.

The Director chapter depicts current responsibilities: capture intent, retain the caller’s conditions, delegate bounded work, communicate what is actually known, evaluate returns and conclude against the whole request. Record icons are conceptual durable records, not a mandated file format.

Learning distinguishes evidence, owner-initiated review, proposal, accepted maintained guidance and later use. Optional growth has its own justification and implementation; a later request tests the genuine new Synthesis responsibility. No automatic learner, background reviewer or compulsory Architect is promised.

The shared exchange is a proposed transport. Each domain keeps ownership and records; the requester evaluates the result. A person explicitly authorizes preparing a schedule draft. The system returns that draft without treating permission to prepare as permission to book a visit or spend money.

## Presenter controls

- **Next / →, Previous / ← or a stage dot:** cue one step from its beginning and hold at Ready afterward. **R** replays the current step; **Home** cues the chapter opening.
- **Play all / Space:** choose continuous playback through the remaining steps in this chapter. A Ready step restarts. During continuous playback the button becomes **Pause**, which freezes the whole system and story; **Continue** resumes continuous playback.
- **Pause motion / Resume motion:** freeze or resume the whole system while keeping the current one-step or continuous mode. Use this pair to pause and resume a single cued step; **Space / Continue** chooses continuous playback instead. A new cue resumes motion for that step.
- A chapter selector/link opens its chosen state at the beginning. With reduced motion, selecting a cue shows its settled state immediately. Space keeps normal native behavior when a button or form control has focus.

This contract was checked against the frozen handlers and the tested presentation paths.
